- **A turn that spends past its budget and then ends before the next poll is now `over_budget`** (KAN-557). Before,
  only a poll during the turn checked its usage, so the last spending went unseen: the turn counted as finished
  and its work went out as a ready PR. hangar now checks the usage once more when the turn, or its output-schema
  repair run, ends. It records `finished over budget (…)` and delivers the work as a draft. Codex, which reports
  usage only at the end, now has its token and cost budgets applied this way, and so do best-of-N attempts. The
  agent's own last message stays the summary, and a question it asked still waits for an answer. The job event
  and GitHub comment now say "over budget" rather than "stopped", since such a turn wasn't stopped.
