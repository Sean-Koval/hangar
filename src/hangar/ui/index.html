Jobs

Work handed to cloud agents by people, services and GitHub. Each job ends as a verified branch and PR; reply to steer it, or open its terminal to work alongside the agent.

Files land next to the repo in ~/.hangar/jobs/<job>/, never in the repo itself.

    Sessions

    Streams of work you come back to. The agent's conversation and the repo are saved after every turn and when a session goes idle; resuming starts a fresh sandbox and picks up where it left off.

    Without a message the sandbox just opens, ready for you or the agent.
